**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Retro Rush app (the "Application") for iOS and Android, created by Kevin Wornath (the "Service Provider"). The Application is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases. It contains no advertising.

**Summary**

*   No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
*   No account or login. We never ask for your name, email address or other personal details.
*   No location data is collected.
*   Your game progress stays on your device.
*   Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

**Data stored on your device**

Your game progress (coins, skins, high score and settings) is stored locally on your device only. It is not sent to the Service Provider. Uninstalling the Application deletes this data, including any coins you have bought.

**In-app purchases**

When you buy a coin pack, the payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your payment details (for example your card or bank account) are entered with Apple or Google, and the Service Provider never has access to them.

To verify purchases and make sure you receive your coins, the Application uses RevenueCat, a purchase-processing service provided by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). When you make a purchase or open the coin shop, RevenueCat receives:

*   a random, anonymous app user ID generated by the Application (not linked to your name or account)
*   purchase information, such as the product bought, price, currency, time of purchase and transaction ID
*   technical information, such as the device's operating system, app version, store country and IP address (needed to establish the connection)

This data is used only to process and verify purchases, prevent fraud, and show the Service Provider aggregated sales statistics. It is not used for advertising or tracking, and it is not sold.

**Third-party services**

These services have their own privacy policies:

*   [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
*   [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
*   [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

**Legal basis (EU/EEA users)**

Purchase data is processed to fulfil your purchase (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for fraud prevention, based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). RevenueCat may process data in the United States; such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

**Data retention**

Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal (e.g. tax and accounting) obligations.

**Your rights**

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Because the Application does not use accounts, we may need your purchase's transaction ID (found in your App Store or Google Play purchase history) to locate your data. To make a request, contact kevin.wornath@gmx.de.

**Children**

The Application is not specifically directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact kevin.wornath@gmx.de and it will be deleted.
